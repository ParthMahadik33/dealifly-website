About Me

About Me

I’m Siddhi, a Growth Marketing Expert with 5+ years of experience helping brands grow, attract the right customers, and turn marketing into revenue.

Most companies already have an internal marketing team. But sometimes, having an independent marketing expert working alongside your team can bring a different perspective, fresh ideas and expertise that helps turn marketing efforts into real growth opportunities.

Not a traditional agency. I work more like an extension of your team — understanding your business, working closely with your existing team, identifying opportunities, and helping execution.

My journey started with my own cybersecurity startup - and it failed.

While growing my own start-up, I realised I had taught myself everything about business development, branding, marketing, customer acquisition, growth, and revenue — basically, what it really takes to grow a business.

Eventually, I realized that the expertise and lessons I gained through my own start-up journey could be utilized to help other businesses grow.

And that’s what I do today.

“My biggest failure became the foundation of my current success - Dealifly.”

Growth marketing expertise, an entrepreneurial mindset, and technical knowledge - the combination I bring to help your business grow.
