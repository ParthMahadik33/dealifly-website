About Me

I’m Siddhi, a Growth Marketing Expert with 5+ years of experience helping brands grow, attract the right customers, and turn marketing into revenue.

Most companies already have an internal marketing team. But sometimes, an independent marketing expert working alongside your team can bring a fresh perspective, new ideas, and expertise to turn marketing efforts into real growth opportunities.

Not a traditional agency. I work as an extension of your team.

My journey started with my own cybersecurity startup — and it failed.

While building it, I realised I had learnt everything about business development, branding, marketing, customer acquisition, growth, and revenue — what it really takes to grow a business.

Now, I utilise the experience and lessons from my startup journey to help other businesses grow. And that’s what I do today.

Turning failure into the foundation of my current success — Dealifly.

Growth marketing expertise, an entrepreneurial mindset, and technical knowledge — the combination I bring to help your business grow.
